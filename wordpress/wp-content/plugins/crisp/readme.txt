== Description ==

Crisp is a Live Chat and AI Chatbot plugin for WordPress that helps you talk to website visitors, answer customer questions 24/7, and manage customer support from one place.

Install Crisp on your WordPress website in a few clicks, with no coding required. Use live chat to engage visitors in real time, automate repetitive questions with AI, and give your team the tools they need to support customers faster.

= Live Chat for WordPress =

**Turn website visitors into conversations and customers.**

Add a fast, customizable live chat widget to your WordPress website and talk to visitors when they need help. Answer questions before they leave, remove friction during the buying journey, capture leads, and support customers directly from your website.

* **Customizable chat widget** → match the chat experience with your WordPress website and brand.
* **Proactive messages** → engage visitors at the right moment instead of waiting for them to contact you.
* **Mobile and desktop apps** → answer visitors and customers wherever you are.
* **Real-time notifications** → know when someone starts a conversation so you can reply quickly.

= AI Chatbot for WordPress =

**Support customers 24/7 without increasing your workload.**

Hugo, Crisp’s AI Agent, works as an AI chatbot that answers repetitive customer questions automatically using your own business knowledge. Give customers instant answers when your team is unavailable, reduce waiting time, and let your team focus on requests where human support makes a difference.

* **Website training** → let Hugo learn from the content already available on your WordPress website.
* **Knowledge sources** → add help articles, files, and other business content to improve AI answers.
* **Custom instructions** → define how your AI chatbot should behave and respond to customers.
* **Human escalation** → automatically hand conversations over to your team when human help is needed.

**Keep every customer conversation under control.**

Bring WordPress live chat, email, WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, and other channels into one shared inbox. Give your team the full conversation context, reduce tool switching, and make sure customers always reach the right person.

* **Private notes** → collaborate with teammates without involving the customer.
* **Conversation history** → understand previous interactions before replying.
* **Assignments and routing** → send conversations to the right teammate or team.
* **Multichannel inbox** → manage customer conversations from multiple channels in one place.

**Help your team resolve customer conversations faster.**

Crisp AI Copilot helps support agents understand requests, write answers, and take over conversations faster. Reduce repetitive work and help your team handle more customer conversations without sacrificing response quality.

== FAQ ==

= How to install Crisp Live Chat on my website? =

Upload the plugin files to the `/wp-content/plugins/crisp` directory, or install the plugin through the WordPress plugins screen directly.
Use the Settings->Crisp screen and click on "Link with Crisp"
If you're getting in trouble, we have a [video about how to install Crisp on WordPress](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qgPN61LTzZM)
